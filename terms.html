Terms of Service

Last revised: 20 November 2026.

1. Service

Grey VPS Runtime is a private personal assistant and data synchronization environment used by its operator and explicitly authorized individuals. It is not a public service and does not offer self-service registration.

2. Authorized use

Only authorized users may connect their Google account or use the automation. Access depends on individually granted permissions. Users must not attempt to access another person's notes, accounts or backup artifacts.

3. Google Drive permissions

Google OAuth access is optional and must be specifically granted by the Google account holder. Authorization can be revoked through Google's account settings. The operator intends to maintain least-privilege Drive access for approved files.

4. Availability and backups

The software is provided as an evolving private system without a guarantee of uninterrupted service, complete historical record recovery or loss-free operation. Encrypted offsite backups improve resilience but are not a substitute for independently tested full-server restoration.

5. Changes and termination

The operator may change or stop private access, subject to applicable law and user privacy rights. Authorized users may request termination or deletion via the developer support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.

6. Contact and privacy

For questions, use the developer support address on the Google OAuth consent screen. Our Privacy Policy explains how Google user data and private backup materials are handled.
